The poster coastline data comes from Natural Earth's public-domain 1:110m land layer, version 5.1.2.

- [Source GeoJSON](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/v5.1.2/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson)
- [Terms of use](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/)

The local copy retains all 127 land features, removes unused attributes, and rounds coordinates to four decimal places. It contains coastline geometry only.
